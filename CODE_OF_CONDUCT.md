# Code of Conduct

We want everyone who participates in Laravel User Presence to have a respectful and welcoming experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and empathy.
- Discuss technical disagreements constructively.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Take responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would make a professional community unsafe or hostile.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern to Evan Schleret at [evan@schleret.ch](mailto:evan@schleret.ch). Reports will be reviewed privately and handled fairly. Depending on severity and context, maintainers may clarify expectations, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove someone from project spaces. The reporter's privacy will be respected.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and its community impact guidance.
